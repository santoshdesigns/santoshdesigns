Hi, I'm Santosh.

AI-Powered Product Designer

I'm an early-career UI/UX and product designer in Hyderabad. I finished my Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science in 2025 (CGPA 6.8).

My interest in design started back in 8th class, when I began experimenting with photo and video editing. I enjoyed creating visuals, trying different styles, and understanding how small changes could make something look and feel better. What started as a simple hobby slowly became a genuine interest in visual creativity and design.

As I explored further, my interest naturally moved from editing visuals to designing mobile and web applications. I became more curious about how people interact with digital products, why certain interfaces feel easy to use, and how design can solve real problems. This led me to learn UI/UX design, user flows, wireframing, prototyping, and visual design.

Today, I’m growing as a UI/UX and Product Designer, combining my visual design background with user research and problem-solving. I enjoy understanding users, researching their needs, and turning those insights into simple, useful, and visually engaging digital experiences.

I also work with AI in my design process, using AI tools for UX and UI design while keeping the thinking and the decisions my own.
